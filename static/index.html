Official Scoring Rules Guide

Rules of Play
Ball (Outside Zone) 4 Balls = Walk (BB)

Pitch outside the strike zone that the batter does not swing at. 4 balls awards 1st base and advances forced runners.

Strike (Called / Swinging) 3 Strikes = Out (K)

Pitch passing through the strike zone or any swing-and-miss. 3 strikes records a Strikeout (+1 Out).

Foul Ball Never Strike 3

Batted ball landing in foul territory. Counts as strike 1 or 2, but can never record strike 3 on an ordinary foul.

Hit By Pitch (HBP) Immediate 1B

Pitch strikes the batter in flight without a swing. Immediate advance to 1st base; forced runners advance.

Ball In Play 8 Outcomes

Batted ball fair into play. Choose Single (1B), Double (2B), Triple (3B), Home Run (HR), Out, Error (E), Sac Fly (SF), or Double Play (DP).
